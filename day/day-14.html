13. Authorising Firms

  • A firm must be authorised (or exempt) to carry on regulated activities in the UK.
  • Authorisation grants specific permissions — a firm may be authorised for some activities but not others.
  • FCA assesses: fitness & propriety, resources, systems/controls, governance, business model.
Exam Trap

“Authorised” is not a blanket licence. The exact permissions matter. A firm advising outside its permissions is acting illegally.

14–15. Appointed Representatives (AR)

What is an AR?
  • An AR can carry on certain regulated activities under the responsibility of an authorised principal firm.
  • The principal firm is responsible for the AR’s conduct and must supervise it.
  • ARs are common in distribution models; FCA has tightened scrutiny (“AR rebooted”).
Key controls (principal firm)
  • Due diligence before appointing AR
  • Ongoing monitoring, training, compliance checks
  • Clear scope of activities permitted

16. FCA Directory

  • Public register of individuals working in financial services who are in scope of SM&CR / certification.
  • Used to verify adviser status and whether a person is authorised to give advice.

17. Market Abuse (high level)

  • Insider dealing, unlawful disclosure of inside information, market manipulation.
  • FCA can investigate and take enforcement action.

18. Responsibilities of Regulated Firms

  • Maintain adequate systems & controls.
  • Ensure competent staff (training & competence).
  • Keep records; manage conflicts; treat customers fairly; comply with rules.

19–20. Adviser Status + Variety of Advice

Independent advice
  • Whole of market + unbiased + considers a broad range.
  • Must disclose independent status clearly.
Restricted advice
  • Limited product range / limited providers / specific area only.
  • Must disclose restrictions clearly to clients.
Exam Trap

“Independent” is a regulated label with requirements. If advice is limited to a panel, it is restricted.

21. Retail Distribution Review (RDR)

  • Reformed retail investment advice (from 2012).
  • Removed commission bias for investment products; introduced adviser charging and clearer disclosure.
  • Raised professional standards.

22–23. Supervising Firms + Enforcement Powers

  • Supervision: ongoing monitoring, thematic reviews, data requests, visits.
  • Enforcement: investigations, fines, public censures, permission restrictions, prosecutions.

24. Mortgage Market Review (MMR)

  • Introduced stricter responsible lending standards after the financial crisis.
  • Focus: affordability assessment, stress testing, advised sales process, interest-only controls.
  • Links strongly into MCOB rules and mortgage exam questions.
Kenneth’s Angle

MMR is basically “credit underwriting discipline” in regulation form: verify income, verify expenditure, stress test, and ensure the repayment strategy is credible.

Memorise

AR model

AR operates under principal firm; principal is responsible + must supervise.

Independent vs restricted

Panel/limited range = restricted.

MMR

Affordability + stress tests + responsible lending.

Practice Questions (MCQ)

Q1. Who is responsible for the conduct of an Appointed Representative (AR)?
A. The AR only
B. The FCA only
C. The principal firm
D. The FSCS
Reveal answer
Answer: C — The principal firm is responsible and must supervise the AR.
Q2. A firm gives investment advice only from a small panel of providers. What type of advice is this?
A. Independent
B. Restricted
C. Execution-only
D. Regulated mortgage advice
Reveal answer
Answer: B — Panel-limited advice is restricted. “Independent” requires broad whole-of-market consideration.
Q3. What is the main purpose of the Mortgage Market Review (MMR)?
A. Increase mortgage commission
B. Strengthen responsible lending and affordability assessments
C. Remove all fixed-rate mortgages
D. Replace the FCA with the PRA
Reveal answer
Answer: B — MMR strengthened affordability and responsible lending requirements.
